QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Does KeenPassword Vault use cloud sync?

No. The app is designed for offline-only storage. Your vault remains on your device and isn't synced or uploaded to a cloud service.

Do I need to create an account?

No. KeenPassword Vault does not require an online account or sign-up to use the app.

Is KeenPassword Vault a subscription?

No. It uses a one-time purchase model, so there are no recurring subscription payments.

Can I back up and restore my vault?

Yes. Backup Manager lets you export and restore your encrypted vault whenever you choose.

What does Freeze Vault do?

Freeze Vault puts your vault into read-only mode, protecting your entries from accidental edits or unwanted tampering.

What is Breach Scan?

Breach Scan checks whether your credentials appear in known data leaks. It is intended as a way to help you identify credentials that may need attention.

Does Breach Scan send my passwords over the internet?

No. Breach Scan preserves your privacy using k-anonymity. Your actual passwords and master keys never leave your device or get sent over the network.

What happens if I forget my master password?

KeenPassword Vault has no password retrieval options. Because your data is encrypted offline, forgetting your master password means your vault cannot be recovered, though you can reset the app by erasing your data. We strongly recommend setting a password hint.
