QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Does KeenPassword Vault use cloud sync?

No. The app is designed for offline-only storage. Your vault remains on your device and isn't synced or uploaded to a cloud service.

Do I need to create an account?

No. KeenPassword Vault does not require an online account or sign-up to use the app.

Is KeenPassword Vault a subscription?

No. It uses a one-time purchase model, so there are no recurring subscription payments.

Can I back up and restore my vault?

Yes. Backup Manager lets you export and restore your encrypted vault whenever you choose.

What does Freeze Vault do?

Freeze Vault instantly locks the vault to prevent edits or changes while it is frozen.

What is Breach Scan?

Breach Scan checks whether your credentials appear in known data leaks. It is intended as a way to help you identify credentials that may need attention.
