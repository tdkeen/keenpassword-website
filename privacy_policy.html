PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

KeenPassword Vault is designed to operate entirely offline. Your data remains on your device and under your control.

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

KeenPassword Vault does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information outside your device.

All data you enter into the application, including passwords, secure notes, and categories, remains local to your device and is stored in your encrypted vault.

Data Storage and Security

Your passwords and notes are encrypted and stored solely on your device.

KeenPassword Vault does not use cloud storage, online backup, or remote synchronization.

You control your data and may delete it at any time through the application's interface or by uninstalling the application.

Internet and Network Usage

KeenPassword Vault does not require an internet connection.

The application does not send, receive, or request data over the internet.

Third-Party Services

KeenPassword Vault does not use analytics, advertising networks, tracking technologies, or third-party services.

Because the application does not transmit data to external servers or third-party services, your vault data is not shared with external parties.

Account Requirements

No account creation, login, or registration is required to use KeenPassword Vault.

All features are available immediately upon installation.

User Control

You may export or import your encrypted vault file manually.

These operations are performed locally on your device and do not involve external servers.

Children's Privacy

KeenPassword Vault does not collect personal information from users, including children, and does not transmit or share personal information with third parties.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be made available on this website and within the application.

Contact Information

For questions or support regarding KeenPassword Vault, please visit the Contact & Support page.
